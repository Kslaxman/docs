# Conclusion and Future Work

Our analysis has demonstrated the viability of H3-based demand forecasting for micro-mobility. Key findings include:
- High spatial precision with H3 Resolution 8 cells.
- Temporal consistency across daily and weekly cycles.
- Strong benchmark performance from Gradient Boosted trees.

## Future Research Directions:
1. Deep Learning Integration: Exploring LSTM and Graph Neural Networks for spatial-temporal dependencies.
2. External Data Sources: Incorporating weather, local events, and public transit schedules.
3. Real-time Optimization: Developing a dynamic fleet redistribution algorithm based on 15-minute ahead forecasts.
