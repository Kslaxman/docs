# Model Training & Evaluation

Moving from clean features to functional prediction weights involves complex partitioning and optimization. `06model_training.ipynb` details the primary pipeline constructed around Tree-Based Architectures (XGBoost, LightGBM) prior to moving to final deep learning states.

## Evaluation Targets
Our system trains specialized predictors for several distinct targets:
- **T1**: Citywide holistic hourly demand (Regression)
- **T3**: Per-H3-Cell microscopic demand (Regression)
- **T2 & T4**: Driver Repositioning and Surge Alert Systems (Binary Classification)

## The Train/Validation/Test Split Scheme

Given the temporal dependency of the dataset, random train-test splitting (K-Fold CV) guarantees data leakage from the "future" to the "past".
We enforce a strict chronological partitioning:
- **Train Window**: E.g., Dec 1 - Dec 22
- **Validation Window**: E.g., Dec 23 - Dec 27 (Handling unique conditions like Christmas Day)
- **Test Window**: E.g., Dec 28 - Jan 1 (For absolute zero-leak validation modeling against the notoriously difficult NYE anomaly)

## Gradient Boosting Benchmarks

### XGBoost vs LightGBM
Both algorithms form our core baseline benchmarks:
- **XGBoost**: Trained for absolute precision focusing on highly engineered lag metrics.
- **LightGBM**: Evaluated specifically because of its internal Leaf-Wise growth strategy resulting in dramatically faster categorical handling and sub-millisecond production inference.

### Optuna Hyperparameter Optimization
Neither model is run on default parameters alone. We instantiated **Optuna** studies to aggressively probe hyperparameter space:
- Searching for optimal Depth, Learning Rate, Tree constraints, and Alpha regularizations.
- Post-training, Optuna visualization highlights that historically lagged trip counts overwhelmingly influence tree splits over any secondary engineered dimension.

### Evaluation Metrics Evaluated:
- **MAE** & **RMSE**: To penalize massive deviations on high-density cells while optimizing general ride scale.
- **R-Squared Score**: For comparing model fit variance across wildly differing geographical zones.
- **Inference Latency**: Strict evaluation requiring predictions to yield under a rigid millisecond latency threshold for real-time app compliance.
